I'm starting this blog because I keep learning things and then forgetting how I got there. I'd rather have it written down, and if it helps someone else, even better.

I'll write about whatever I'm working on or learning at the moment: projects, CTFs, and the tools that give me trouble. Some of it will be rough. When I get stuck, I'll say where and share how I got out, because that's the part I wish more people wrote about.

There will also be a lot of nice photos of my dog, Charmin!
